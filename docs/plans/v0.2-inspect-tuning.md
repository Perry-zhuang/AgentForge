General adjustment: a four-or-more-character Chinese phrase receives the full bounded source score, while a Java architecture-role bonus requires either multiple concepts or one specific term of at least three characters. ## R6 — Core-token coverage and strong-seed propagation

P1.1 separates core keywords, exact phrases, and derived n-grams. Core matches are evaluated per logical source line; two or more core concepts receive a multi-core bonus and complete coverage receives an additional bonus. Derived n-grams are capped at five points. Controller and Service contribute two points, Repository and Mapper one point, and Component zero.

P1.2 narrows the ranking model to structural, semantic, dependency, and responsibility evidence. One-hop propagation now starts only from structural task matches, dependency points are auxiliary, and propagated evidence never creates another seed. Passive files without structural task identity are capped at LOW, while structurally matching models such as task-named request types remain eligible for normal ranking.

P1.3 freezes that ranking model and calibrates confidence independently. A Spring Controller or Service with at least two matched core tokens can be MEDIUM when its score is at least 20; a structural task match can be MEDIUM from 15; HIGH additionally requires a score of at least 40 and strong structural evidence. Component-annotated integration classes and unannotated service interfaces are not promoted merely because their filenames look executable. Passive-file caps always take precedence. The CLI now shows match coverage and a concise confidence reason, omits zero-point evidence, groups positive evidence, and exposes only meaningful derived phrases.

For `历史告警`, broad files that matched only `历史` fell from MEDIUM to LOW or disappeared. Exact phrase and full-coverage files moved ahead of layer roles. For `分析备份历史告警同步`, per-line co-occurrence removed large enum/constant false positives that previously assembled all four concepts from independent declarations.

The engine does not translate Chinese core tokens into English identifiers and does not infer interface implementations or method calls. Consequently, an implementation containing only an English method such as `getHistoryAlert` may rank below its Chinese-documented interface. This is an explicit P1.1 limitation rather than a repository-specific exception.
